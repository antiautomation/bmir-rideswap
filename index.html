📖 How This App Works

  • This app runs in real-time while connected. New posts will appear without refreshing.
  • Please be sure to choose your Direction before posting your seat/request!
  • Please be sure to include information about your pickup and drop-off location in the notes!
  • You can provide either an email or phone number (or both) for contact information.
  • Use the Location/Destination field to specify your city, and the Details field for additional information.
  • For rides From Burning Man, include your playa location (e.g., "at 9:00 & Esplanade") in the details.
  • Use the Day and Location filters to find specific rides.
  • Click the flag button (🚩) to report listings that should be removed or are no longer available.
  • You can edit or delete your own posts using the buttons that appear on your listings.
  • Contact information (email/phone) appears as clickable links for easy communication.
  • Session Key: When you first visit, you'll get a unique session code. Take a screenshot to save it - you'll need this code to edit or delete your listings from other devices.
  • Cross-Device Access: Use your session code to access your listings from any device. Enter the code in the session input field to regain access to your posts.
  • Session Management: Your session code is displayed in the header. Click "Change" to enter a different session code if needed.
