📖 How This App Works

  • Session Code: Save your unique session code (take a screenshot) - you'll need it to edit/delete listings from other devices.
  • Direction: Choose "To Burning Man" or "From Burning Man" before posting - this is crucial!
  • Contact Info: Provide email or phone (or both) for ride coordination.
  • Location Details: Include pickup/drop-off specifics in the Details field, especially playa location for "From Burning Man" rides.
  • Real-time Updates: New posts appear automatically without refreshing.
  • Filters: Use Day and Location filters to find specific rides.
  • Flagging: Click 🚩 to report inappropriate or outdated listings.
  • Editing: Use the edit/delete buttons on your own listings.
  • Cross-Device: Enter your session code on any device to access your listings.
  • Interactive Features: Click phone numbers to send text messages, click email addresses to compose emails, and click city names to open Google Maps with directions to Black Rock City.
